# BIB Form Builder v8.3 — Single-Column Enterprise Themes

## Change

The SAP-reference and Maximo-reference public form themes retain their v8.2 visual styling while restoring the form field flow to a single column.

- SAP theme: one form field per row; classic compact ERP label/control styling is preserved.
- Maximo theme: one form field per row; Carbon/Manage-inspired flat field styling is preserved.
- Mobile behavior remains single-column.
- Workflow execution observability and the redesigned submission result screen are unchanged from v8.2.
